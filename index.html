Jane Pan

I'm a PhD student at New York University, advised by He He. I study machine learning and natural language processing, and I'm broadly interested in understanding, evaluating, and improving how large language models are used — including human-centered NLP and human–AI collaboration. I'm supported by the NSF Graduate Research Fellowship and a DeepMind Fellowship.

I'm currently a Visiting Researcher at FAIR Alignment, where I work with Jack Lanchantin, Swarnadeep Saha, and Jason Weston.

Previously, I completed my Master's at Princeton University, advised by Danqi Chen, in the Princeton NLP Group, and graduated magna cum laude from Columbia University with a B.S. in Applied Mathematics. Before NLP, I worked in quantitative finance and data science at Morgan Stanley and Acadian Asset Management.
